<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

<p>This report is published as a self-contained embedded document. It uses a dedicated <strong>Field Ledger</strong> aesthetic (a soil-and-canopy almanac with warm parchment ground, forest-green primary and harvest-ochre accent) designed specifically for agricultural technology and food security.</p>

<p>The argument runs from framing to evidence to implication: global production is not the binding constraint on food security; distribution of the tools to grow, store and move food is. The report presents four inline charts (undernourishment trend, 2050 demand arithmetic, maize yield gap, post-harvest loss) and a five-lever schematic from farm to plate, with all data attributed inline and full sources at the end.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-read">How to read</h2>

<p>Read the embedded document top to bottom. Use <a href="/reports/agtech-food-security-digital-report/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open full screen ↗</a> for an unobstructed reading view. The report closes on a binding term that frames how food-security policy should be read going forward.</p>

**Binding term:** Read food-security policy as a distribution problem, not a production problem: measure success by how many farms can deploy the tools that already exist, not by how many new tons the global margin can produce.
