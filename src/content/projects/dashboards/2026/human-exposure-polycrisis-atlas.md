<p>A fixed-viewport interactive cartographic console mapping <strong>human exposure to polycrisis</strong> across 175 inhabited territories from 2000 to 2100. Rather than viewing climate stress, environmental pollution, resource scarcity, sovereign debt fragility, and conflict in isolation, the dashboard models how simultaneous threshold breaches amplify one another—and how institutional, infrastructural, and clean-transition buffers dampen cascade transmission.</p>

<p>Use the top bar to switch across four IPCC socioeconomic and warming pathways (<strong>SSP1-2.6 Resilient Convergence</strong>, <strong>SSP2-4.5 Middle of the Road</strong>, <strong>SSP3-7.0 Polycrisis Escalation</strong>, and <strong>SSP5-8.5 Fossil Extremes</strong>) or inspect individual map layers, including the composite <strong>Polycrisis Exposure Index (PEI)</strong>, <strong>Air, Water &amp; Chemical Pollution Burden</strong>, <strong>% Population Exposed</strong>, and <strong>Compound Sync Amplification</strong>. Click or search (<code>⌘K</code>) any nation to open its Country Intelligence Dossier with a seven-domain radar profile, 2000–2100 multi-scenario trajectory, and pathway divergence table. Press <code>Space</code> or scrub the docked bottom timeline to animate the century across <strong>Atlas</strong>, <strong>Equal Earth</strong>, and interactive 3D <strong>Globe</strong> projections.</p>

<h2 id="method">Method and sources</h2>

<p><strong>Composite index &amp; compound synchronization.</strong> For each territory <em>c</em>, year <em>t</em> (2000–2100), and pathway <em>s</em>, seven domain stress scores (0–100) are weighted—Climate &amp; Thermal Extremes (20%), Food &amp; Water Scarcity (16%), Air, Water &amp; Chemical Pollution (15%), Macro-Fiscal Fragility (14%), Geopolitics &amp; Conflict (13%), Health System Strain (11%), and Infrastructure Coupling (11%)—and combined with a nonlinear <strong>Compound Synchronization</strong> bonus triggered whenever three or more domains concurrently exceed critical stress (54+), minus an <strong>Adaptive Resilience Buffer</strong> capturing governance, clean energy/cooking adoption, pollution controls, and infrastructure capacity.</p>

<p><strong>Air, water &amp; chemical pollution.</strong> The pollution domain integrates ambient PM₂.₅ and wildfire smoke, household air pollution from solid biomass cooking, contaminated freshwater and untreated wastewater, plastics, and industrial/agricultural chemical runoff. Under SSP1-2.6, accelerated electrification, clean cooking diffusion, and wastewater treatment drive steep reductions in pollution exposure by 2100, whereas SSP3-7.0 and SSP5-8.5 compound fossil combustion, wildfire particulates, and unmanaged runoff with rising thermal stress.</p>

<p><strong>Empirical anchors &amp; scenario caveat.</strong> Historical trajectories (2000–2025) are calibrated against UN World Population Prospects (WPP 2024), IIASA SSP demographic multipliers, WHO/IHME Global Burden of Disease PM₂.₅ and household air pollution estimates, Yale Environmental Performance Index (EPI) water/pollution benchmarks, ND-GAIN vulnerability profiles, the INFORM Risk Index, IPCC AR6 CMIP6 regional sensitivities, and observed 2008–2009 and 2020–2022 global shock pulses. Post-2025 values are structured scenario projections for comparative systemic analysis rather than point forecasts.</p>
