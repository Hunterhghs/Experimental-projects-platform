Human Exposure to Polycrisis (2000–2100)

A polycrisis arises when multiple global systemic hazards cross critical thresholds simultaneously, producing nonlinear second- and third-order cascades whose human impact exceeds the sum of isolated shocks. This interactive atlas models national and population-weighted exposure across 175 inhabited territories from 2000 to 2100 under four IPCC Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSPs).

PEI(c, t, s) = compress( Σ wₖ·Dₖ(c,t,s) + λ(s)·Sync(D₁..₇) − 0.25·(R(c,t,s) − 35) )

Seven Hazard Domains (wₖ)

  • Climate & Thermal Extremes (20%): Wet-bulb heatwaves, drought/flood volatility, coastal & cyclone hazard.
  • Air, Water & Chemical Pollution (15%): Ambient PM2.5 & wildfire smoke, household biomass cooking smoke (HAP), contaminated freshwater, plastics & chemical runoff.
  • Food & Water Scarcity (16%): Aquifer depletion, staple yield variance, calorie import & bio-resource fragility.
  • Macro-Fiscal Fragility (14%): Sovereign debt distress, inflation pass-through, trade concentration.
  • Geopolitics & Conflict (13%): Armed conflict proximity, forced displacement, chokepoint exposure.
  • Health System Strain (11%): Climate- and pollution-sensitive disease burden and surge capacity deficit.
  • Infrastructure Coupling (11%): Grid reliability, clean energy/water gap, rapid urbanization stress.

Compound Sync & Resilience

  • Synchronization Amplification: Captures pairwise co-stress energy whenever 3+ domains concurrently exceed critical stress (54+), scaled by scenario fragmentation factor λ(s).
  • Adaptive Resilience Buffer (R): Institutional governance, clean energy & clean cooking adoption, wastewater/pollution controls, and fiscal headroom dampen cascade transmission.
  • Empirical Anchors: Historical period (2000–2025) integrates UN WPP 2024 demographics, WHO/IHME GBD PM2.5 & HAP estimates, Yale EPI water/pollution metrics, ND-GAIN, INFORM Risk Index, IPCC AR6 CMIP6 sensitivities, and 2008/2020 global shock pulses. Post-2025 values represent scenario projections.
